# Adepta Sororitas Faction Pack FAQ Notes (2026-04-05)

This note records Adepta Sororitas faction-pack FAQ clarifications validated against the latest official `ADEPTA SORORITAS` faction pack PDF available on 2026-04-05.

## Implemented Clarifications

- `Litany of Deeds` can re-roll the Miracle dice gained from `Acts of Faith` when a friendly `ADEPTA SORORITAS` unit is destroyed within 12" of a friendly `Imagifier`.
- `Litany of Deeds` can also re-roll each additional Miracle die generated by `Saintly Example` before those dice are added to the Miracle Dice pool.
- `Verse of Holy Piety` remains active for the bearer's unit until the start of the next battle round even if the enhanced `CHARACTER` is destroyed after activating the vow.
- A Miracle die can be used on a re-roll if the unit has not already performed an `Act of Faith` that phase.
- If a re-roll is made after substituting a die with a Miracle die, the original Miracle die is lost and the unit cannot use another Miracle die for that same prior `Act of Faith`.
- `Simulacrum of the Ebon Chalice` increases the number of `Acts of Faith` a unit can perform in a phase, but it still only allows one die to be substituted in a single roll.
- The `Acts of Faith` destruction-triggered Miracle die is gained immediately when the unit is destroyed, not delayed until the end of the phase.

## Engine Notes

- `Verse of Holy Piety` now persists its active vow on the attached-unit root so the FAQ outcome still holds after an attached bearer is removed mid-round, including `PRECISION` cases that leave a bodyguard unit behind.
- Additional regression coverage was added for Miracle-dice re-roll timing, `Saintly Example` plus `Litany of Deeds`, and the `Simulacrum of the Ebon Chalice` single-roll substitution limit.
